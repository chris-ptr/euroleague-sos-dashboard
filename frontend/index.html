EuroLeague Strength of Schedule

Net Rating & Win% based schedule difficulty, updated automatically each round

Project overview

This dashboard analyzes Strength of Schedule (SoS) in the EuroLeague by combining team performance metrics (Net Rating, Win%), the official EuroLeague schedule, and upcoming opponent difficulty. The goal is to contextualize team performance: how difficult has a team's schedule been, and how difficult will it be going forward?

Data sources

Schedule — parsed from the official EuroLeague Regular Season PDF into EL_2025_26_EL_RS_Schedule.csv, used for forecasting future opponent difficulty.

Team stats — fetched via the open-source euroleague_api wrapper, providing OffRtg, DefRtg, NetRtg, standings and results.

Core metrics

NetRtg = OffRtg − DefRtg

OffRtg: points scored per 100 possessions. DefRtg: points allowed per 100 possessions. Higher NetRtg ⇒ stronger team.

Strength of Schedule — Win% based

OW% = mean(OppW%)  ·  OOW% = mean(OW% of opponents)
SoS(Win) = (2 · OW% + OOW%) / 3

Weights direct opponent strength more heavily while reducing noise via secondary opponent metrics.

Strength of Schedule — Net Rating based

OppNetRtg = mean(NetRtg of opponents)  ·  OONetRtg = mean(OppNetRtg of opponents)
SoS(Net) = (2 · OppNetRtg + OONetRtg) / 3

Less sensitive to close-game variance and more stable early in the season than the Win% method.

How to read the tabs

  • Next-N Games SoS — forecasts upcoming schedule difficulty using the next N opponents; color-coded by opponent NetRtg.
  • SoS vs Team NetRtg — contextualizes performance against schedule difficulty; quadrants highlight over/underachievers.
  • NetRtg & Win% Table — compares the two SoS methods side by side for every team.

How this stays up to date

A scheduled job recomputes and publishes new rounds automatically as EuroLeague games finish — there's no manual update step.
