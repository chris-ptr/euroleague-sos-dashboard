Project overview

This dashboard analyzes Strength of Schedule (SoS) in the EuroLeague by combining team performance metrics (Net Rating, Win%), the live EuroLeague schedule, and upcoming opponent difficulty. The objective is to contextualize team performance by answering: how difficult has a team's schedule been, and how difficult will it be going forward?

Data sources

Schedule (live API) — upcoming fixtures come straight from the official EuroLeague schedule endpoint, so postponed games and home/away swaps are reflected automatically. This replaced a hand-maintained CSV exported from the Regular Season PDF, which had to be re-exported by hand whenever the calendar moved.

Team stats (euroleague_api) — historical performance data fetched via the open-source euroleague_api package by Giannis Giasemidis, powering our possession-based efficiency metrics and historical SoS calculations.

Core metrics

Measures points differential per 100 possessions:

\[\text{NetRtg} = \text{OffRtg} - \text{DefRtg}\]

Where OffRtg = points scored per 100 possessions and DefRtg = points allowed per 100 possessions. Higher NetRtg ⇒ stronger team, lower NetRtg ⇒ weaker team.

Strength of Schedule (formulas)

Adapted from the Hack-a-Stat methodology and applied to EuroLeague datasets.

OppW%: opponent winning percentage  ·  TmGP: team games played  ·  OppNetRtg: opponent net rating

Opponents' Winning Percentage (OW%)

\[\text{OW\%} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{OppW\%}_i}{\text{TmGP}}\]

Opponents' Opponents' Winning Percentage (OOW%)

\[\text{OOW\%} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{OW\%}_i}{\text{TmGP}}\]

Strength of Schedule — Win% based

\[\text{SoS}_{\text{Win}} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{OW\%} + \text{OOW\%}}{3}\]

This formula weights direct opponent strength more heavily while reducing noise via secondary opponent metrics.

Opponents' Net Rating (OppNetRtg)

\[\text{OppNetRtg} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{NetRtg}_i}{\text{TmGP}}\]

Opponents' Opponents' Net Rating (OONetRtg)

\[\text{OONetRtg} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{OppNetRtg}_i}{\text{TmGP}}\]

Strength of Schedule — Net Rating based

\[\text{SoS}_{\text{Net}} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{OppNetRtg} + \text{OONetRtg}}{3}\]

Efficiency-based SoS is less sensitive to close-game variance and remains more stable early in the season.

Four Factors

Dean Oliver's Four Factors break scoring efficiency into the four things a team actually controls, in descending order of how much they decide games. They answer a different question from Strength of Schedule — what kind of team is this, rather than how hard has it had it — so the Four Factors tab stands on its own and feeds none of the SoS numbers.

Shooting the ball — Effective Field Goal %

\[\text{eFG\%} = \frac{\text{FGM} + 0.5 \cdot \text{3PM}}{\text{FGA}}\]

Field goal percentage that credits a three for the extra point it is worth.

Taking care of the ball — Turnover Rate

\[\text{TOV\%} = \frac{\text{TOV}}{\text{FGA} + 0.44 \cdot \text{FTA} + \text{TOV}}\]

Share of possessions ending in a turnover. Lower is better.

Offensive rebounding — Offensive Rebound %

\[\text{OR\%} = \frac{\text{OREB}}{\text{OREB} + \text{Opponent DREB}}\]

Share of a team's own misses it recovers.

Getting to the foul line — Free Throw Rate

\[\text{FTR} = \frac{\text{FTM}}{\text{FGA}}\]

How much free-throw scoring a team generates per shot it takes.

The DEFENSE half of the table is the same four formulas applied to what opponents did against that team: the eFG% they were allowed to shoot, the turnovers they were forced into, the free throws they were sent for. The defensive rebounding column is the mirror of the opponents' OR% — the share of their misses this team cleaned up.

Every ratio is built from summed season totals rather than averaged game by game, so a high-volume game counts for more than a low-volume one — the same weighting the possession-based ratings use.

Four Factor Rating

The eight columns collapse into one number per team using Oliver's relative weights — 40% shooting, 25% turnovers, 20% rebounding, 15% free throws. The factors are in incompatible units (eFG% spans a few points, FTR is a ratio around .25), so each is first standardized across the league at that round, then signed so positive is always good for the team, then weighted:

\[\text{4F} = \underbrace{0.40 z_{\text{eFG}} - 0.25 z_{\text{TOV}} + 0.20 z_{\text{OR}} + 0.15 z_{\text{FTR}}}_{\text{offense}} + \underbrace{\left(-0.40 z_{\text{eFG}}^{opp} + 0.25 z_{\text{TOV}}^{opp} + 0.20 z_{\text{DR}} - 0.15 z_{\text{FTR}}^{opp}\right)}_{\text{defense}}\]

Because it is built from z-scores, the rating is relative by construction: it says how far from league average a team sits in units of league spread. That makes it comparable between teams within a round, but not across rounds — a +1.5 after round 10 and a +1.5 after round 38 are not the same achievement. Cell shading works the same way: each column is colored against its own league mean, with green always the good end, so the shade means "better or worse than the field on this factor".

How to read the tabs

  • Next-N Games SoS — forecasts upcoming schedule difficulty using the next N scheduled opponents; color-coded cells represent opponent strength (NetRtg).
  • SoS vs Team NetRtg — contextualizes team performance against schedule difficulty; quadrants highlight overachievers, underachievers, and schedule outliers.
  • NetRtg & Win% Table — compares the NetRtg-based and Win%-based SoS estimates side by side for every team.
  • Four Factors — a standalone team profile, independent of schedule difficulty: what each team does well and badly on each side of the ball. It does not feed any SoS number.
Glossary

Team efficiency metrics

  • OffRtg: points scored per 100 possessions
  • DefRtg: points allowed per 100 possessions
\[\text{OffRtg} = \frac{\text{Points Scored}}{\text{Possessions}} \times 100\]
\[\text{DefRtg} = \frac{\text{Points Allowed}}{\text{Possessions}} \times 100\]

NetRtg: efficiency differential per 100 possessions

\[\text{NetRtg} = \text{OffRtg} - \text{DefRtg}\]

SoS metrics (Win% based)

OppW%: individual opponent win rate  ·  OW%: average opponent win rate

\[\text{OW\%} = \frac{1}{\text{TmGP}} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{OppW\%}_i\]

OOW%: average win rate of opponents' opponents

\[\text{OOW\%} = \frac{1}{\text{TmGP}} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{OW\%}_i\]

SoS (Win%): composite win-rate based difficulty

\[\text{SoS}_{\text{Win}} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{OW\%} + \text{OOW\%}}{3}\]

SoS metrics (NetRtg based)

OppNetRtg: average opponent efficiency

\[\text{OppNetRtg} = \frac{1}{\text{TmGP}} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{NetRtg}_i\]

OONetRtg: average efficiency of opponents' opponents

\[\text{OONetRtg} = \frac{1}{\text{TmGP}} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{OppNetRtg}_i\]

SoS (NetRtg): composite efficiency-based difficulty

\[\text{SoS}_{\text{Net}} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{OppNetRtg} + \text{OONetRtg}}{3}\]

Four Factors

  • eFG%: effective field goal percentage — three-pointers credited at 1.5 field goals
  • TOV%: share of possessions lost to a turnover (lower is better on offense, higher is better on defense)
  • OR%: share of a team's own missed shots it rebounds
  • DR%: share of the opponents' missed shots it rebounds
  • FTR: free throws made per field goal attempted
  • 4F Rating: the eight factors weighted 40/25/20/15 as league-relative z-scores
Implementation notes & roadmap
  • Visualization layer: Vega-Lite (built with Altair, rendered client-side with vega-embed)
  • Assets: team logos served as static PNGs, referenced by URL from the chart specs
  • Data pipeline: a local script computes new rounds and writes precomputed chart JSON into the site itself; this page fetches it from the same origin — no live backend, no database
  • Current scope: EuroLeague (competition code E); seasons available are listed in the Season selector. Future updates may include EuroCup support and further historical seasons.
